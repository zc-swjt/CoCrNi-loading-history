# Data Sources and Physical Definitions

The study data were generated by molecular dynamics of two independently assigned equiatomic CoCrNi chemical configurations. Each configuration was subjected to P0, P1 and P2 histories in two mixed-mode convergence families, giving twelve simulations. The loading lasts 84 ps with a 1 fs timestep at 300 K. Within each family the histories reach the same prescribed stress-intensity factors and share the subsequent loading from 70 to 84 ps.

The interatomic potential is the CrCoNi NEP model associated with Wu et al., *Communications Materials* 7 (2026), 245, [doi:10.1038/s43246-026-01245-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43246-026-01245-3). The exact parameter file included here has SHA-256 `98e4e98e84007b794506c9e0c0ef5993beec19aff27bb667850fbbec35e142b7`. Its upstream model revision is `85bd041d66c012dbc3ccc355728b98cb47f7b274`.

The specimen contains 445760 atoms, including 58240 prescribed-boundary atoms. The crack tip is at x=y=200 angstroms. The z direction is periodic with period 70.7442 angstroms. Cubic axes [100], [010] and [001] coincide with specimen x, y and z. The elastic constants are C11=290.73116264133336, C12=184.239628864 and C44=144.54858537955556 GPa.

The 30-degree family's initial mixities are 30, 0 and 60 degrees for P0, P1 and P2; the 45-degree family's initial mixities are 45, 15 and 75 degrees. An actual `loading_history.csv` records time_ps, KI_MPa_sqrt_m, KII_MPa_sqrt_m, K_MPa_sqrt_m and psi_deg. The analysis reads KI and KII from the relevant simulation, including the 45-degree paths.

Stacking-fault classifications and dislocation lines are extracted from the saved atomic configurations with OVITO. `positions_A` and `reference_positions_A` contain N-by-3 coordinates in angstroms. `fault_type` uses the planar-fault classification, with 2 denoting intrinsic faults and 3 denoting twins. Intrinsic fault-area weights are sqrt(3)*d^2/4, where d is the local interatomic distance; twins use twice this factor. The regional definitions are r<5 nm, 5<=r<13 nm and r>=13 nm. Line records preserve points, tangent orientation and spatial Burgers vectors from the dislocation analysis.

Local stresses use the per-atom configurational virials evaluated on complete saved specimens with the same potential. `virial_eV` has shape N-by-3-by-3. Stress is minus the symmetrized virial sum divided by N*a^3/4, with a=3.53721 angstroms; conversion to GPa uses 160.21766208. The reported sectors are the forward 1-2 and 2-3 nm regions with polar angles from -30 to 30 degrees. Kinetic stress is not included. The incremental elastic reference starts from the actual 70 ps stress and uses the subsequent changes in both KI and KII.

Dislocation glide is measured from corresponding reference-mapped lines, including periodic z alignment. Signed swept area divided by participating line length and time interval gives mean glide velocity. The local virtual work uses the measured line geometry, a fixed-end tapered in-plane displacement, and the actual mixed-mode field. It combines applied work, stacking-fault energy and anisotropic line energy. The adopted rigid intrinsic stacking-fault energy is the mean of three potential-based branches, -0.05290915409588749 J/m2. The shared logarithmic line-energy factors are 2, 3 and 4. The DXA Burgers/tangent convention uses the calibrated Peach-Koehler multiplier -1.

This document identifies how the physical quantities were obtained. It is not a repository of all raw trajectories or numerical result tables.
